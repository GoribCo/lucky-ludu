# Fold Blitz: The Dice Gambit

**Player Count:** 2+
**Setup Time:** 5 minutes
**Game Type:** Resource Management, Luck, Simple Strategy
**Goal:** Be the first player to fill every slot across all of your assigned Folds.

---

## 🎲 Components Needed

*   **Dice:** One standard six-sided die (D6).
*   **Player Boards:** One board/sheet per player, structured as $F$ separate "Folds."
*   **Tokens/Markers:** A distinct marker for each player (or a pen/pencil).
*   **Player Setup Card:** (Optional) A sheet listing the current player order.

## 📝 Setup Phase

1.  **Determine Player Count (N):** All players agree on $N$.
2.  **Determine Number of Folds (F):** All players agree on $F$.
    *   *(Guideline: A reasonable starting range for F is 3 to 5. If the game is getting too long, decrease F. If it 
is too easy, increase F.)*
3.  **Board Preparation:** Each player receives a board with $F$ slots. Each slot is labeled 1 through 6.
4.  **Starting Player:** The player who last rolled a die starts first.

---

## ⚙️ Core Gameplay Mechanics

### The Fold Board

*   **Structure:** A single player's board is comprised of $F$ distinct folds.
*   **Slots:** Each individual fold has 6 slots, representing the numbers 1 through 6.
*   **Objective:** To fill all $F$ folds completely. This means filling every single slot on your board.

### The Turn Sequence

A player's turn is a series of linked rolls. A turn only ends when the dice roll result cannot be placed anywhere on 
the player's board.

**Turn Steps:**

1.  **Roll:** The active player rolls the D6, resulting in a number $R$.
2.  **Placement Check:** The player checks their board for any empty (unfilled) slots marked with the number $R$.
3.  **Placement Action:**
    *   If there is at least one available slot for $R$, the player **must** place the rolled marker $R$ into *one* of 
the available slots.
    *   If there are *no* available slots for $R$, the turn ends immediately.

4.  **Continuation:** If the player still has open slots, they *must* take an optional action (Strategy Phase) to 
determine if they roll again.

---

## ✨ Strategic Options (The Choice)

When a player successfully places a roll $R$ and their turn has not yet ended, they must choose one of the following 
options. These options dictate whether they continue rolling or end their turn.

### 1. Standard Roll (The Safe Play)
*   **Action:** The player simply rolls the D6 again.
*   **Benefit:** Low risk, high reliability. This is the best choice when the player knows they have many open slots, 
or if they have a wide variety of numbers missing.
*   **Constraint:** If the new roll lands on a number that is already filled in all $F$ folds, the turn ends 
instantly.

### 2. Focus Roll (Targeted Strategy)
*   **Action:** The player declares a specific number $T$ (1–6) that they wish to fill next. They do **not** roll the 
dice.
*   **Mechanic:** The player immediately places the marker $T$ into *one* of their available slots matching $T$.
*   **Benefit:** Guarantees progress on a specific number. Excellent for filling a critical slot (e.g., completing a 
row or a column).
*   **Constraint:** The player cannot use this action if the number $T$ they choose has no available slots anywhere on 
their board.

### 3. Risky Gambit (The Power Play)
*   **Action:** The player chooses to forgo their next roll, but instead steals one random slot number $X$ from the 
currently *most advanced* opponent (the opponent closest to winning).
*   **Mechanic:** The player places a marker $X$ on a random, currently empty slot on their *own* board, as if they 
had rolled $X$. They then announce that the opponent they targeted loses one turn turn-count (or simply skip the next 
player after them).
*   **Benefit:** Disrupts opponents and gives immediate board advantage.
*   **Constraint:** The player cannot use this option if they have filled a significant portion of their board (e.g., 
if less than 20% of their slots are open, they cannot risk it).

---

## 🏆 Winning and Scoring

**Winning:**
The first player to successfully fill the very last slot on their board wins the round.

**Scoring (Tracking the Finish Order):**
After a winner is declared, players score points based on their finishing position:

*   **1st Place:** 10 points
*   **2nd Place:** 8 points
*   **3rd Place:** 6 points
*   **4th Place:** 4 points
*   *(Etc., decreasing by 2 points per place)*

The game continues until one player reaches a predetermined point total (e.g., 50 points), or a set number of rounds 
are completed.

---

## 💡 Gameplay Tips & Considerations

*   **Block Opponents (Indirectly):** While you cannot directly block opponents, by focusing on specific numbers 
(using Focus Roll), you force the dice rolls of other players toward the numbers you still need, making their rolls 
less efficient.
*   **The Corner Strategy:** If you have several folds, try to fill all the 1s, all the 2s, etc. If you complete a 
number (e.g., all six '1' slots are filled across your folds), you have achieved a local mini-victory and can focus 
your strategy on the next number.
*   **Knowing When to Use the Gambit:** Only use the Risky Gambit when you are confident that your next two turns will 
lead to a win. If the opponent is far behind, the gambit might only delay the inevitable.
